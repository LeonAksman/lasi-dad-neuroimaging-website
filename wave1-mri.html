LASI-DAD conducted an N=137 pilot MRI study between 2018 and 2020 during its Wave 1 data collection.
Originally planned as an N=200 study, the pilot study was cut short due to the Covid-19 pandemic.

Scanning took place at three sites:
1. Karnataka (National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences; NIMHANS, Bangalore)
2. West Bengal (Institute of Neurosciences, Kolkata)
3. Maharashtra (NM Medical Center, Mumbai)
