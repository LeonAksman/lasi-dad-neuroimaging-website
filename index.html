Cohort & Acquisition Overview

The LASI-DAD MRI Sub-Study provides multi-site 3-Tesla structural, functional, and diffusion neuroimaging data collected from older adults (aged 60+) across India as part of the Harmonized Diagnostic Assessment of Dementia for the Longitudinal Aging Study in India (LASI-DAD). Acquisition sequences are harmonized with the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI-3) protocol across participating clinical scanning centers to support cross-national dementia, vascular brain injury, and environmental aging research.

De-Identification & Privacy Safeguards: All DICOM files have been converted to BIDS-compliant NIfTI format with direct personal identifiers, institutional tags, and exact acquisition dates scrubbed from headers. All 3D structural scans (T1w and T2-FLAIR) have been processed with a validated facial-feature removal pipeline (pydeface) prior to repository ingestion.

Linkage with LASI-DAD Cognitive, Clinical & Exposure Data

Neuroimaging files in XNAT are indexed using anonymized LASI-DAD participant identifiers so approved researchers can link brain MRI phenotypes with cognitive battery scores, clinical consensus dementia diagnoses, sociodemographic variables, and environmental exposure measures. Non-imaging survey and clinical variables are distributed separately through the official LASI-DAD / Gateway to Global Aging Data Portal under standard LASI-DAD data access procedures.

Step-by-Step Access Procedure

Controlled Access Policy: To protect participant privacy and uphold Indian and US institutional ethics approvals, LASI-DAD MRI files are not available for anonymous bulk download. Access is granted per modality tier following Data Access Committee review of a signed Data Use Agreement (DUA).
  1. Create an XNAT User Account: Register for an account on the LASI-DAD XNAT Archive and record your exact XNAT username.
  2. Submit the MRI Data Use Agreement (Below): Provide your institutional credentials, XNAT username, intended research abstract, ethics/IRB status, and select the specific MRI modality projects required for your analysis.
  3. Committee Review & Provisioning: Applications are reviewed for scientific scope and DUA compliance (typical turnaround: 5–10 business days). Upon approval, your XNAT account is added as a read-only Collaborator on the approved modality project(s).
