Welcome! This site describes the neuroimaging component of the Harmonized Diagnostic Assessment of Dementia for the Longitudinal Aging Study in India (LASI-DAD). The overall goal of LASI-DAD is to study late-life cognition and dementia in India. LASI-DAD collects an extremely rich set of cognitive, health, lifestyle, environmental and biomarker data.

More information on LASI-DAD, including how to access its trove of publicly available data, is available here.

As part of LASI-DAD Waves 1 and 2 (and as planned for Wave 3), high-quality brain imaging data has been or will be collected using 3 Tesla MRI scanners at multiple sites across India. Please check out the tabs that describe the imaging in each wave. Check out the ‘Data Requests’ tab for info on how to access imaging data from each wave.
