Wave 1 MRI Data

Data from the LASI-DAD Wave 1 MRI pilot study are available through the Image and Data Archive (IDA) online database hosted by the Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI) at the University of Southern California.

Permission to access the LASI-DAD IDA site can be requested by contacting Sarah Gao (gaosarah@usc.edu) from the LASI-DAD study team.


The following data is available within the IDA (#1 and #2 below are available as downloadable spreadsheets in the Download -> Study Data section):

1. Freesurfer-based regional brain volumes and cortical thicknesses. Note that these measures are provided 'as is': they were derived from T1-weighted scans by the neuroimaging team but not thoroughly quality controlled (QC'ed) or harmonized by the team (in contrast to the Wave 2 Freesurfer measures, which have been carefully QC'ed and harmonized across sites using NeuroCombat).

3. Raw DICOM data for all modalities.
